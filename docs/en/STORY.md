# The Blackwire legend (in the project owner's words, 25.10.2026)

**The player.** Formerly an employee of a large corporation. Took out a loan and could not repay it; the bank took half of the possessions from the house.
What is left is a home computer, an old router and the debt. This is where the game begins: an empty desk, messages in the mailbox.

**The hello-world group.** A hacker group that spent years in the shadows. After a major hack it has returned — to show that it still
has strength left, and that it is ready to stand up for the ordinary outcasts of a totalitarian society.

**Entering the story.** In the mailbox the player finds a message with a mysterious link — it leads into the group's hidden network of sites (`.hw` domains,
gateway `gate.hw`). The link with a one-time invitation code opens a page where the group explains who they are and sets the first
trial. From there the player has to go through the group's missions (the second stage — content).

**What of this belongs to the first stage (the computer's ecosystem):**
- the player's mailbox with the initial messages: the bank (debt, seizure of possessions), the former employer (dismissal), the provider,
  spam, the hello-world message with the link `http://gate.hw/invite/<code>`;
- the hello-world organisation in the generator (OrgKind::Underground) with a hidden network of sites: `gate.hw` (`/`, `/invite/<code>`, `/manifesto`,
  `/board` — the job board, a stub for now), the group's mail address;
- the "Mail" app (Inbox, Sent, reading, replying, composing, links open in the browser);
- the browser opens organisations' sites and the hidden network.

**Second stage:** the group's missions (a chain of missions with condition checks), reply messages, story development, vulnerabilities and hacks,
surveillance by security centers and the cyber police, money and debt.
